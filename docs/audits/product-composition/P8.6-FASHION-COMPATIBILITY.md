## 3. Specific incompatibilities

### 3.1 Company stock hydration — critical

On the target branch:

- `Product::booted()` syncs company stock from the product mirror;
- `CompanyStockService::hydrate()` loads the active company's stock onto a product;
- `InventoryService::applyMovement()` hydrates after locking the product;
- `CostResolver` reads company stock for average/last purchase cost.

P3 currently locks products and reads `Product::stock_quantity` directly. On the target branch that scalar may represent another company or a stale mirror. Porting P3 unchanged would:

- preflight against the wrong stock,
- allow or reject the wrong company's consumption,
- snapshot the wrong cost.

Required: inject/use `CompanyStockService` in the fashion port and hydrate every locked product before preflight and cost resolution. The same applies to production finished-goods stock and reversal.

### 3.2 Idempotency uniqueness — high

Source migrations create global unique keys:

- `sales.idempotency_key`;
- `returns.idempotency_key`;
- `composition_runs.idempotency_key`;
- `production_orders.idempotency_key`;
- `production_orders.reversal_idempotency_key`.

Target branch scopes business document numbers and financial event keys by company. A global idempotency unique would let one company's retry collide with another company's request. Required target migration:

- add `company_id` to runs/production/recovery where missing;
- replace global unique keys with `(company_id, idempotency_key)` for company-owned documents;
- keep source-branch migration 001/011 compatible during upgrade, then drop/recreate on target;
- ensure null-company console behavior is explicit.

### 3.3 Company ownership on composition children

Parent product ownership is not enough:

- `product_compositions`: belongs to product; add `organization_id` or enforce product organization.
- `product_composition_items`: inherit composition organization; validate component belongs to same organization.
- `composition_runs`: company-owned; add `company_id`, and ensure `sale_item_id`/sale company matches.
- `composition_run_items`: inherit run; no direct company ID initially, but raw reports need join/filter.
- `return_component_recoveries`: company-owned; add `company_id`, guard return/sale item/run item.
- `production_orders`: company-owned; add `company_id`, guard product organization and composition.
- `inventory_transactions`: already company-owned on target; every new movement writes active company.

### 3.4 COGS and reports

Every raw query that touches composition/production must get a company predicate:

- `CogsCalculator::summary`, `lineCostsForSaleItemIds`, `returned`, `returnedQuantityByItem`;
- `ProductionReportService::componentUsage` and `productionHistory`;
- inventory/financial/day-book queries that aggregate production movements;
- product/stock reports that count produced quantity.

Otherwise a company can see another company's composition consumption or production cost.

### 3.5 Locations

Neither branch has a location/warehouse stock table. The target uses a location string on products. P3/production store a location snapshot only. Do not describe this as location-isolated stock. If fashion requires store/warehouse isolation, that is a separate architecture phase.
