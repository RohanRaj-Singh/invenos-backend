## 6. New P8 behavior verification

### Production reversal valuation

Test sequence:

1. produce 1 unit at cost 5;
2. change raw component cost to 10;
3. produce a second unit at cost 10; finished average is 7.5;
4. reverse the first production.

Expected and verified:

- stock returns to 1;
- average becomes 10;
- stock value remains 10;
- no component restoration;
- original run snapshot remains.

### Uncosted finished stock

Test sequence:

1. produce 1 unit from one known-cost and one unknown-cost component;
2. production is incomplete, average remains null, `uncosted_qty = 1`;
3. sell the unit.

Expected and verified:

- sale item `cost_complete = false`;
- `uncosted_quantity = 1`;
- `cost_price = null` because no known cost exists;
- COGS is 0 but explicitly incomplete;
- `uncosted_qty` returns to 0.

### Mixed costed/uncosted line

- 2 units sold from 1 costed unit at 10 and 1 uncosted unit;
- line cost is 10 known, unit cost 5, `cost_complete = false`;
- COGS reports 10 (known subtotal), not 20 and not 0.
